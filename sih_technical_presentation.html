LIFECYCLE & EXECUTION ENGINE

End-to-End Technical Workflow: From Login to Settlement

Step-by-step transaction lifecycle through Supabase PostgreSQL, Realtime Bidding, and Dual-OTP verification

ESCROW DISTRIBUTION 88% Worker • 6% Welfare • 3% Society • 3% Tech
PHASE 01 Supabase Auth

Real Auth & Strict Role Isolation

User registers via Supabase Auth as either Citizen or Worker. Metadata is saved to profiles and cryptographically locks routing: Citizens only see Customer Portal, Workers only see their console.

PHASE 02 PostgreSQL Insert

"Name Your Price" Bidding Post

Citizen describes problem, selects SOS 30-min urgency, and sets an initial budget (e.g., ₹350). Record is inserted into job_requests with secure 4-digit Start/Completion OTPs.

PHASE 03 WebSockets Pub/Sub

Direct Fair-Wage Dynamic Negotiation

Supabase Realtime pushes job alerts to nearby workers within 3 km. Workers submit counter-offers (e.g. ₹400 with 15 mins ETA). Offers appear live on the citizen's screen without reloading.

PHASE 04 Escrow Engine

Deal Lock & 88/6/3/3 Binding

Customer accepts Worker’s ₹400 offer. Server locks status to EN_ROUTE and calculates split: Worker: ₹352 (88%), Welfare: ₹24 (6%), Society: ₹12 (3%), Platform: ₹12 (3%).

PHASE 05 Security Gate

Cryptographic Dual-OTP Handshake

Technician arrives at doorstep. Citizen shares 4-digit Start OTP (7412). Worker enters code into app to start the official job timer. Eliminates fake arrivals and disputes.

PHASE 06 Welfare Settlement

Settlement & PMSBY Ledger

Worker marks job finished with proof photo. 88% is credited to worker wallet, 6% is auto-deposited into the PMSBY ₹2 Lakh insurance pool, and +₹25 is added to the micro-pension ledger.

Q1: Why use Supabase instead of a custom Node server?

Answer: Supabase provides production-grade PostgreSQL ACID compliance, built-in Row-Level Security (RLS), and Realtime WebSockets out of the box. This prevents financial race conditions during escrow calculations and allows our dynamic bidding engine to scale across thousands of concurrent devices in $<100\text{ms}$.

Q2: Why Direct Dynamic Negotiation over fixed algorithmic gig rates?

Answer: Fixed pricing leads to on-site surprise quotes and job refusals. Dynamic bidding creates a fair market price: citizens set their budget, and nearby verified workers compete on price and arrival speed. Once agreed, the price binds an immutable 88/6/3/3 split with zero hidden platform cuts.

Q3: How do you guarantee safety inside citizen homes?

Answer: Two-tier trust: DigiLocker Aadhaar KYC + Skill India trade certification endorsed by a legal, brick-and-mortar Cooperative Society. Cryptographic Start OTP prevents fake technician check-ins, and in-app masked calling protects customer privacy.
